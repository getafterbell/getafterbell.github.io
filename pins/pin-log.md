## Rules for new pins

- 1000 × 1500 PNG made from `pin-template.html` in the same navy-and-gold style. Check every render for overflow before posting.
- No prices (Amazon's rules). No health claims beyond what the site's guides say. Nothing that tells teens to use adult supplements.
- Every description ends with "I earn from qualifying purchases."
- Write for anyone who trains. Teen and high school angles are fine for some pins, but most should be general.
- Don't repeat a topic already in the posted table below unless it's a clearly new angle.
