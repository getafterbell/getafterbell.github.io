## Account checklist

- Use a free **Pinterest business account** so you get analytics.
- **Claim the website** (Settings → Claimed accounts). The verification tag is already on the site.
- Put the site link and a one-line bio on your profile, for example: "Gear and free guides for high school athletes."
- Check **Analytics** each week for outbound clicks, and make more pins like the ones that get clicked.
